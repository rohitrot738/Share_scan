# Training Case 002 — TVS Supply Chain Solutions

## Label
Successful multi-timeframe reversal / expansion move.

## Screenshots supplied
1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1H, 1D, 1W, 1M.

## Behavioural DNA observed

### Higher timeframe context
- Long decline from much higher prices into a broad bottoming region.
- Repeated stabilization around the 90–120 region before recovery.
- 1D/1W structure shows a transition from persistent markdown into basing/recovery.
- Price later approaches an old overhead-supply area around the mid-130s/140 region, so late entries must receive an overhead-supply penalty.

### Mid timeframe setup
- 30m/1H show price compressed around roughly 120–124 before the expansion.
- Downside progress weakens before the move.
- The move begins with large range expansion and strong relative-volume expansion.

### Lower timeframe trigger
- 1m/5m show a quiet/low-activity phase before the move.
- First expansion leg breaks the local balance.
- Price pauses/shallowly retraces instead of fully giving back the impulse.
- A second expansion leg follows, producing a staircase move.
- Volume expands with the impulse and remains elevated while price accepts higher levels.

## Generic pattern family extracted
BOTTOMING_COMPRESSION_TO_STAIRCASE_EXPANSION

Sequence:
1. Prior markdown / negative drift.
2. Selling-pressure exhaustion and reduced downside efficiency.
3. Local base / volatility compression.
4. Relative-volume expansion.
5. First bullish displacement.
6. Shallow pause or higher-low hold.
7. Second expansion / continuation.
8. Late-stage overhead-supply and chase-risk check.

## Scanner lessons
- Do not wait for the stock to already be +10% before issuing the first useful signal.
- EARLY/WATCH should appear during compression/exhaustion before the first major expansion.
- READY should require first displacement plus acceptance/hold.
- CONFIRMED requires continuation quality and multi-timeframe agreement.
- DO_NOT_CHASE/LATE penalty should increase after multiple ATRs of extension, vertical volume climax, or when price runs directly into old higher-timeframe supply.
- This case must complement, not replace, the Mazagon impulse-base-breakout family.

## Anti-overfitting rule
No absolute TVS price levels are hard-coded into detection logic. All thresholds are expressed using ATR, percentage range, rolling volume, local structure and higher-timeframe supply distance.
